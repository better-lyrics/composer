Mel-Band RoFormer vocal model by Kimberley Jensen
=================================================

Composer's optional "High quality vocals" separator runs the Mel-Band RoFormer
vocal model trained by Kimberley Jensen (GitHub KimberleyJensen, Hugging Face
KimberleyJSN). The model card lists the weights under the MIT License.
